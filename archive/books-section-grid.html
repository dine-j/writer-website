Curse of Nohvan

Welcome to Variska

Dark Epic Fantasy

A simple escort mission.
A cursed valley.
A stranger he was never meant to protect — and now can't abandon.

Read more

They came for coin and pies; now this cursed land may very well eat them alive.

Nohvan, a talented hunter, and his companions—a quiet paladin, a volatile mage, an earnest bard and a feathered wanderer—are trapped in Variska after a simple escort mission turns into a demon hunt. When Aurelia arrives like a mirror of someone Nohvan loves, he must protect her from the vampire ruling the valley and find a way out of this hell.

Between the hungry creatures lurking in the night and dangerous alliances, protection comes at a cost and not every bargain is worth taking... Nohvan must decide what he will give—their trust or the only kind of family he has left.
